Common Questions

Everything You Want to Know

Does acupuncture hurt?

Most patients feel little to no pain. The needles used are extremely thin, much finer than a hypodermic needle. You may feel a mild sensation of pressure, warmth, or tingling at the insertion point, which is normal and typically subsides quickly.

Is acupuncture safe to combine with my other treatments?

Yes. Acupuncture works well alongside physical therapy, chiropractic care, massage, and conventional medicine. Xavier works collaboratively and can coordinate with your other providers to ensure your care plan is cohesive.

What should I do before my first appointment?

Come hydrated and wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing, as Xavier may need to access areas that are typically covered. Bring any questions you have, and take a moment beforehand to think about what you're hoping to get out of treatment, whether that's recovering from an injury, managing pain, reducing stress, or simply feeling better. Xavier will build your sessions around your goals.

Do you accept insurance?

We are currently a private-pay practice. We provide detailed superbills that you can submit to your insurance provider for potential reimbursement. Many HSA/FSA plans cover acupuncture, so check with your provider.

I'm not an athlete. Can I still be treated here?

Absolutely. While we specialize in sports acupuncture, our treatments benefit anyone dealing with pain, stress, or chronic conditions. If you have an active lifestyle or simply want to feel better and move more freely, you're in the right place.
